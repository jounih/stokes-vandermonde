### Contrast with Lagarias (1999) / Li's RH Positivity Criterion
- **Li's criterion / Lagarias (1999) / Bombieri–Lagarias (1999)** formulates RH as an exact equivalence with positivity ($\lambda_n \ge 0$ for all $n \ge 1$, or positivity of Weil's explicit-formula quadratic form), where even a single off-line zero breaks positivity at large order.
- By contrast, the **centered Cramér spectral kernel** $K(x) = \sum_{\mathrm{Im}\,\rho > 0} \mathrm{Re}(e^{-\gamma x})$ ($\rho = 1/2 + i\gamma$, paired by $\rho \leftrightarrow 1 - \bar\rho$) and all shifted Stieltjes Hankel matrices of $K(\sqrt{y})\,dy$ are **strictly positive unconditionally — WITHOUT RH or GRH!** Because $|\mathrm{Im}\,\gamma| < 1/2$ forces $\cos((\mathrm{Im}\,\gamma) x) \ge 0$ on $0 < x \le \pi$, any additional positive real low zeros in `γ_low` add strictly positive terms $e^{-\gamma x} > 0$, and on $x \ge \pi$ the exponential gap $e^{-(H - h)\pi}$ crushes the entire tail regardless of sign, strict positivity of these Stieltjes moment Hankel matrices holds even if arbitrarily many off-line zeros exist above height $H$.
